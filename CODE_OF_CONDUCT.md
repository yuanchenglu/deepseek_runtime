# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in DeepSeek Runtime must remain professional, evidence-based, and respectful. Contributors are expected to:

- critique code, designs, claims, and evidence rather than people;
- state uncertainty and distinguish static analysis from executed verification;
- protect credentials, private prompts, checkpoints, and vulnerability details;
- accept technical disagreement without harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- make project spaces accessible to people of different backgrounds and experience levels;
- avoid spam, deceptive claims, undisclosed conflicts of interest, and coordinated abuse.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private information, deliberately exposing secrets or vulnerabilities, sabotaging project infrastructure, and persistent disruption after a maintainer instruction.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where sensitive, and based on observable behavior. Security reports must follow `SECURITY.md` rather than public discussion.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project-controlled communication channels, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers through a private channel where available. If no private channel is available, open a minimal public issue requesting private contact and do not include personal, credential, or exploit details.
